03 / LETTERS
UNSENT · 01

Dear you,

I don't know if there is still a place for these words in your life.

Maybe there doesn't need to be. Some letters are written not to be delivered, but simply because the heart needed a quieter way to speak.

I hope you're doing well. I hope life has been kind to you in the ways I no longer get to see.

And I hope, wherever we are now, we both became a little more like the people we once wanted to be.

— F.
